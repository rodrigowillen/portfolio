# Obsidian Pure Black Design Specification

## Overview
A high-precision, brutalist-editorial developer portfolio designed on a 100% pure black (`#000000`) canvas. Rejects generic AI SaaS templates, aurora blobs, and artificial glassmorphism in favor of typographic mastery, surgical 1px micro-borders, and tactile interaction.

## Colors
- **Pure Black (`#000000`)**: Canvas base for true OLED blacks, maximum contrast, and zero visual noise.
- **Surface Elevation (`#09090b`, `#121214`)**: Subtle depth layering with zero saturation.
- **Precision Borders (`#1c1c1f`, `#3f3f46`)**: Hairline 1px borders for structure and focus.
- **Accents**: Monochromatic high-contrast white with terminal emerald (`#10b981`) reserved strictly for live status indicators. Zero purple/violet (Purple Ban compliant).

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Space Grotesk` - bold geometric letterforms with industrial tech personality.
- **Body**: `Inter` - ultra-readable neutral grotesque font engineered for screens.
- **Data & Microcopy**: `JetBrains Mono` - technical authenticity for badges, specs, and metrics.

## Layout & Architecture
- Asymmetric editorial hero with interactive developer terminal.
- Spec-sheet layout for skills and tech stack.
- Precision cards with spotlight border highlight on hover.
- Privacy-first contact portal via direct form (zero exposed personal emails or phone numbers).

## Do's and Don'ts
- **DO**: Maintain 100% pure black backgrounds.
- **DO**: Use hairline 1px borders with subtle transitions.
- **DO**: Prioritize typography and structural rhythm.
- **DON'T**: Use purple, violet or neon cyan gradients.
- **DON'T**: Reintroduce generic floating gradient blobs.
- **DON'T**: Display visible personal contact info outside the form.
